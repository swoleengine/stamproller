# Stamproller Roll Designer

Design your own text rings for the **Stamproller** marker roller stamp, right in the browser, and download a printable STL.

**Open the designer:** https://swoleengine.github.io/stamproller/

- Text, symbols, your own logo (PNG/SVG), freehand drawing
- 10 / 20 / 30 cm rolls or custom circumference, negative or standard style
- Live checks for thin spots and gaps, paper preview and 3D preview
- 45° slopes so the TPU ring prints upright without overhangs
- Download the TPU ring (optionally with the PETG hub)

Everything runs locally in your browser – nothing is uploaded.

Stamproller on MakerWorld: https://makerworld.com/de/@swoleengineer · YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/@joeldoing
